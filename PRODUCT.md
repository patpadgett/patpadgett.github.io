# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: flat static HTML/CSS generated by a small Python build script (Markdown + YAML front matter in, one HTML file per post out), deployed to GitHub Pages from /data/pat/2_PUBLISHED/blog.patpadgett.com. Chosen because every other patpadgett.com property is flat HTML on Pages, the grime95 generator proves the pattern, and Hugo 0.72 is the thing being retired. User said "your call, just make it work with GitHub Pages."

## Users

Primary: a general reader who likes a good story, arriving from a link, a search result, or one of Pat's other sites. They may know nothing about telecom or about Pat. Success is that they finish a post and click another.

Secondary (confirmed as a side effect, not the lead): hiring managers and recruiters checking Pat out for billing-mediation, infrastructure or DevOps roles. They arrive from work.patpadgett.com or a résumé link and read career posts. They need to find the technical depth quickly without the front page being a résumé.

## Product Purpose

blog.patpadgett.com is Pat's writing home: 125 drafted posts (about 900 words each, dated every three days from 2026-09-21 through 2027-09-26) telling stories from a 30-year life in telecom billing mediation, Unix, open source (corkscrew), music (bass, producing, The Posies, Andrew W.K., Indonesian and Southeast Asian music), small-business web work, Missouri, Indonesia, Tampa Bay, and his current projects (grime95.com, the Octavitin picture book). It replaces a one-post Hugo blog from 2018. It exists so the stories get told in his voice and so the writing itself becomes the strongest signal of who he is.

## Positioning

A working engineer's blog written like a Facebook post from a funny friend: first person, direct address, spec-bragging, affectionate blame, swearing where it is the funniest word, and a punchline instead of a moral. Nobody else has this exact combination of subjects (Nortel AMA records and dangdut koplo; corkscrew and Steve Fisk) and nobody else writes them in this register. The voice is documented in /data/pat/websites/blog/VOICE/VOICE.md and grounded in his real posts in VOICE/facebook.txt.

## Operating Context

- Source posts: /data/pat/websites/blog/drafts-voiced/NN-slug.md (voiced, canonical) with originals in /data/pat/websites/blog/drafts/. Front matter: title, date, slug, description, tags.
- Posts contain bracketed [PAT: ...] placeholders where only Pat knows what happened. A post is not publishable until its placeholders are filled or the sentence cut. The build must refuse or flag any post still containing "[PAT:".
- Publishing cadence: one post every three days, dated ahead; the build should publish only posts whose date is on or before the build date so the site fills in over the year (same pattern as the grime95 publisher).
- Deploy: git push to the blog.patpadgett.com repo; GitHub Pages serves it. No server-side anything.
- Reading happens mostly on phones from social links; long-form 900-word posts must be comfortable at 390px.
- Sister sites that must be cross-linked in the footer: patpadgett.com, work.patpadgett.com, music.patpadgett.com, octavitin.patpadgett.com, grime95.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Must have: post pages, front page listing, tag pages, an About page, RSS (index.xml, the URL the old Hugo site used), sitemap.xml, robots.txt, 404.html, favicon set, canonical URLs, Open Graph and Twitter cards with a 1200x630 image, Blog and BlogPosting JSON-LD, all SEO-floor checks used on the other properties.
- Must not have: Disqus or any comments, Hotjar, jQuery, Font Awesome, Google Fonts or any third-party font CDN, newsletter signup, analytics beyond what Pat adds himself (GA4 G-RBGRC7W946 and Clarity ykj8y83i8t exist on the other sites; include hooks, off by default until he says).
- Preserve the existing URL for the 2018 post if it is kept, else 301 via a meta refresh page, since Pages has no server redirects.
- Career posts (106-113, 115) are written at a quieter register (dial 6) than the rest (dial 8); the design should not need to know this, but tag pages should let a recruiter find "career" and "mediation" posts in one click.
- Decided by user during init: general reader first, hiring a side effect; career posts publish at dial 6, everything else at dial 8; no newsletter, no comments.
- Undecided: whether the 2018 "Awesome Setup" post stays live or becomes a footnote inside post 125.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: blog.patpadgett.com. Author byline: Patrick Padgett (Pat).
- Voice: VOICE/VOICE.md is binding for copy on the site itself (about page, empty states, 404, footer). American spelling. Double-hyphen dashes, never em dashes.
- Family: sits in the patpadgett.com visual family (the hub's woodgrain-TV world; grime95's 1995 terminal world) as a sibling, not a copy. Visual world is decided later in new-work, not here.
- Photos of real people other than Pat require his OK before use.

## Product Principles

1. The story is the product. Nothing on a post page competes with the text; navigation, tags and cross-links wait until the reader is done.
2. Sound like Pat everywhere. Site copy follows VOICE.md; a 404 page that reads like a corporate template is a defect.
3. Never publish a hole. [PAT:] placeholders block the build; better a shorter site than an invented memory.
4. Fill in over time. The site is designed to look alive at 5 posts and at 125; the front page must not look empty on launch day or endless a year later.
5. Flat and boring underneath. One HTML file per URL, no runtime dependencies, works with JavaScript off, deploys with git push.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Long-form reading on phones is the primary case: 16px+ body text, 60-75 character measure, WCAG AA contrast, no light-grey-on-white body copy, visible focus, real headings, alt text on every image. Pat has mobility limitations and reads on a phone; large tap targets and no hover-only affordances.
